# Quality manual

Acme Precision quality system summary. Applies to every job unless a customer
requirement is stricter.

## First article and in-process inspection

Inspect the first piece of every job against the drawing before running the
batch. During a production run, inspect every 25th part, plus the last part.
Record the results on the job traveler.

## Customer requirements

Cobalt Medical: every shipment must include a certificate of conformance and the
material certs (mill test reports) for the lot. Parts are bagged individually.

Bluefin Marine: SS316 parts are passivated after machining. Include the
passivation cert with each shipment.

Northwind Robotics: standard inspection; send the first-article report with the
first shipment of a new revision.

## Nonconforming parts

Put a red hold tag on any part that fails inspection and move it to the
quarantine shelf. The quality manager reviews it within 24 hours and decides:
rework, scrap or use-as-is with customer approval. Log it as an NCR.

## Gauges and calibration

Calipers and micrometers are calibrated every 6 months; the CMM once a year.
Don't use a gauge with an expired calibration sticker.
